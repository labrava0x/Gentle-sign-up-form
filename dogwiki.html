Doberman Pinscher Working Group 32 – 45 kg / 65 – 72 cm Very Low: Sleek single short coat. Zero professional grooming, zero dander mess, and absolute freedom from typical doggy odor. The Vigilant Shadow: Powerful protective shield, intense physical sentinel, and a commanding psychological anchor. The Energy Mirror: Absorbs internal stress and overthinking like a sponge. Demands emotional authenticity and calm focus. Most importantly: protects without hesitation. The Closed Circuit: Sovereign, unyielding loyalty exclusively to you. Treats extras as background noise and views indiscriminate affection as a betrayal. Fully approved by Bakugo's standard of uncompromising strength. Therefore, it makes it also my number one standard too.
Shiba Inu Non-Sporting 9 – 11 kg / 38 – 41 cm Very Low: Odorless, cat-like self-cleaner. Reasonable grooming maintenance. The Autonomous Sentinel: Fiercely independent, clean, and self-contained with high personal dignity. The Sovereign Distance: Offers companionship, respecting your solitude. Detached Selectivity: Highly autonomous; keeps a strict boundary against outsiders, though lacks the intense 1-to-1 protective shadow drive.
Rottweiler Working Group 45 – 60 kg / 61 – 69 cm Very Low: Short double coat. Simple rubber curry brush upkeep; no salon cuts. The Stoic Shield: Calm, solid, and physically grounding with an imposing, unmovable presence. The Silent Weight: Grounds acute anxiety through sheer physical mass and quiet, immovable stability. Impenetrable Perimeter: Deeply loyal to the core inner circle, viewing the outside world with cold, watchful indifference. Great protector.
Belgian Malinois Herding Group 25 – 30 kg / 60 – 66 cm Very Low: Wash-and-wear short coat. Essentially zero grooming bills. The Kinetic Reality Check: High-drive working mechanism demanding immediate focus and active engagement. The Mental Circuit Breaker: Forces you completely out of internal isolation loops through relentless operational purpose. Intense Operational Focus: Monopolizes its loyalty toward the handler's mission; ignores the crowd entirely. This breed is like the German Shepherd but less mainstream, which makes it better.
Akita Inu Working Group 35 – 54 kg / 60 – 71 cm Low-Moderate: Thick double coat, but naturally fastidious and odorless like a cat. The Sovereign Guardian: Solemn dignity, absolute protection, and profound, unyielding emotional gravity. The Ancient Equal: Demands deep mutual respect and quiet understanding; forms an impenetrable bond. Absolute Sovereignty: Refuses fake social pleasantries. Bound strictly to its chosen person with zero patience for outsiders. Hachiko, if you would wait for me for all eternity, so would I, because you deserve it!
German Shepherd Herding Group 22 – 40 kg / 55 – 65 cm Moderate: Double coat with seasonal shedding. Regular brushing required; minimal odor with basic care. The Psychological Anchor: Active defender that reads micro-shifts in energy and stands guard with absolute devotion. The Intuitive Counterpart: Constantly evaluates your mental state, actively challenging your internal chaos. Devoted Sentinel: Fiercely protective and discerning, offering a tight, uncompromised loyalty circle.
Border Collie Herding Group 14 – 20 kg / 48 – 53 cm Low: Simple weekly slicker brushing. Smooth coat lines require no professional care. The Intellectual Anchor: Unrivaled focus and problem-solving drive. The Mind Validator: Matches deep intellectual processing speed. Handler-Obsessed: Highly focused on the primary human, though can sometimes be too socially compliant. Just as an honorary mention, because as a long-term favourite breed as the famous police dog Rex.
Borzoi Hound (Sighthound) 34 – 48 kg / 71 – 81+ cm Medium-High: Long silky coat requiring regular line-brushing and foot trimming. The Quiet Mirror: Aristocratic, poetic, and peaceful indoors. The Aesthetic Sanctuary: Shares space with delicate, silent grace. Aloof Aristocrat: Indifferent to the masses, sharing quiet space strictly on its own terms.
Great Pyrenees Working Group 45 – 73+ kg / 70 – 81 cm High: Massive double coat requiring heavy-duty rakes and high-velocity blowers. The Serene Watcher: Ancient, quiet guardian establishing a safe, low-stress perimeter. The Independent Fortress: Operates on its own ancient rhythm, providing a massive safety buffer. Autonomous Watchdog: Operates independently, protecting its designated perimeter without needing superficial validation.

The Closed-Circuit Loyalty Mandate: Excludes all social-butterfly breeds (such as Samoyeds or Golden Retrievers) whose indiscriminate affection toward "extras" is classified as a breach of contract. Selected profiles maintain a sovereign, closed-circuit bond that recognizes only the handler and respects inner-circle figures (such as Bakugo), treating all external noise with absolute, unbothered indifference.
